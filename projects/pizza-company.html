Digitalizing The Pizza Company

Peer-led group (no team leader) · Management Information Systems Course

An analysis of current operations and a proposed digitalization concept for a restaurant chain, focused on the reservation, ordering, and real-time kitchen coordination experience.

My Contribution

  • Proposed the concept for several subsystems: floor-plan-based table reservation, in-table online ordering, order routing with kitchen synchronization, and real-time preparation tracking.
  • Did not build the interfaces directly, but served as the main advisor shaping the design decisions behind the final product.
  • Reviewed content and slides prepared by teammates. As the team worked without a designated leader, my role leaned toward shaping ideas and providing critical feedback.
