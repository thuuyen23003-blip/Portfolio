Learning Analytics on Odoo ERP

Graduation Thesis · Management Information Systems

Development of a Learning Analytics module integrated into Odoo ERP to analyze learning data, predict student outcomes, and support personalized learning paths.

My Contribution

  • Defined the project's objective and scope: predicting learning outcomes and enabling personalized learning paths for students.
  • Established the functional requirements and key design decisions, including the selection of Random Forest after a comparative evaluation against Decision Tree, and the approach to processing simulated data.
  • Conducted testing, evaluated results, and iteratively refined the solution across multiple development cycles.
  • The technical implementation (Odoo module and model training pipeline) was carried out with the assistance of AI tools, under my direct guidance and review.
Odoo ERPPythonScikit-learnPostgreSQLDecision TreeRandom Forest

Architecture & Design

The module was built as a layer on top of Odoo's existing eLearning data (courses and learner activity), adding a processing pipeline that computes engagement metrics and feeds them into a Python-based model trained outside of Odoo.

Interface

On the Odoo side, instructors and administrators view results through a set of list and detail screens: an overview of all learners with at-risk flags, plus a per-learner breakdown of progress, predicted outcome, exam results, and suggested next steps.

Model Results

Two classification models were trained to predict whether a learner is on-track or at-risk. Random Forest was selected after comparing both models on accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score; a separate clustering step was used to sanity-check the results against natural learner groupings.
